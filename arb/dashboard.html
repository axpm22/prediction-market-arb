Errors & warnings — recent

Everything at WARNING or above from the live logs, newest first. The ANJ block on Polymarket shows here as one fast-fail line per host per poll.

Cumulative settled P&L

One line per (class, lane, variant), cumulative over the window, dated by the day each bet SETTLED. Drawn from the same rows the daily report sums — the panel computes no P&L of its own.
The same headline class on its three bases: optimistic fills, confirmed fills (a missed confirmation counts $0, never dropped), and markout — optimistic minus adverse drift, favourable drift never credited.

Open positions — marked

Every bet with no terminal event, marked at the latest persisted bid minus the taker fee that selling into it would cost. “no quote” is a position we cannot mark, not a position worth zero. WHALE rows carry the whales that formed the signal and the exit rule’s live verdict.

Lane verdicts — latest bench

The most recent arb bench result per lane. NULL is a result, not a failure: it retires a lane. A row past the staleness window is still the answer, just an old one.

L14 whale-consensus lane

REVIEW lane, never headline. Bounds are one-sided (§12): lb80 is the 20th percentile of the event-clustered bootstrap, ub90 the 90th.

Traders — where each wallet is good

Read-time diagnostics over the tape's resolved history, never a lane input. excess% is the win rate above the break-even the price itself implied — success above average given the risk taken. ROI at risk is dollar-weighted return on the capital actually staked; t is the edge over its own standard error. Hygiene-excluded wallets are ranked and flagged, never hidden. Click a column to sort, a row to see that wallet's best markets.

Kill rules — L14 (§12)

Pre-registered before the data. “not yet” means the rule cannot fire at this n; “armed” means it can and has not; FIRED stops booking.

Lane timing

Configured cadence vs the last poll each lane actually persisted. A lane running late is the instrument censoring itself.

Daily schedule

Supervisor-owned slots (config timezone), next firing first.

Today’s ledger
